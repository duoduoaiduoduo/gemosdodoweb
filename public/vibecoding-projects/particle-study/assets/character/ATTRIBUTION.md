# Reference boy character

This project-created skinned character is adapted from public-domain CC0 source meshes and skin textures:

- **Eli — 3D Person, Child age 6 standing**, Innerscene, adapted from MakeHuman / MPFB CC0 assets.
  https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/eli-3d-person-child-age-6-standing-bcaf5944
- **Human base mesh with editable 53-bone rig**, Innerscene, based on MakeHuman / MPFB CC0 anatomical mesh and game-engine skeleton.
  https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/human-base-mesh-with-editable-53-bone-rig-8e7c8ab1

Both source pages dedicate the assets to **CC0 1.0 Universal**:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Project adaptations include child proportions, semantic bind skeleton, full weighted legs and hands, loose off-white cotton shirt, olive shorts, woven garment albedos, rough gray shoes and rubber soles, a reworked dark-brown hair cap and opaque curved hair locks. The reference video itself is not redistributed in this site.

`reference-boy.glb` is an uncompressed glTF 2.0 binary with embedded PNG textures and skinning weights. `reference-boy-rest.json` records the final bind skeleton in metres, Y-up and +Z forward. Foot soles are at y = 0.13 to align with the project's separate skateboard.
